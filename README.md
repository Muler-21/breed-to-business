# Public African, European and Asian sheep genotypes (PLINK)

2,818 sheep × 28,210 SNPs (Ovine SNP50 subset), including 18 Ethiopian populations.

Source: Da Silva A. et al. (2024) History and genetic diversity of African sheep: contrasting phenotypic and genomic diversity. *Animal Genetics*. Data on figshare: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.26731378.v2 (licence CC BY 4.0). Mirrored unchanged; please cite the original authors and the studies that generated each breed's genotypes.
