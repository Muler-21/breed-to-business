How to read this

sourced The diseases and breeding priorities come from the cited Ethiopian studies. expert Standard crop-science knowledge, to be checked with the national commodity programme before you rely on it. The altitude and rainfall envelopes are planning guides, not limits: always confirm with a local adaptation trial.

The variety ranking uses only what the national Crop Variety Register states for each variety (altitude and rainfall range, disease reaction, yield, maturity). 8 registered varieties are loaded now; the full register (about 1,500 varieties) loads when the scraper workflow has run. Where a crop has no varieties loaded yet, the finder still gives the crop guidance.

Ranking points: altitude inside the variety's range 3 (within 200 m: 1); rainfall inside 3 (within 150 mm: 1); priority: up to 3 more for a matching disease, drought, maturity, quality or yield statement. Read the disease reaction yourself: the register uses words like "tolerant" and "moderately resistant" that a simple score cannot weigh.
